## Primary controls

***Arm button (Left trigger):*** This button must be held when moving motors or actuators.

***Arm actuators (Left loystick):*** Moving joystick up/down lifts/lowers arm actuators.<br>
***Bucket actuator (Left joystick):*** Moving joystick left/right lifts/lowers buckey actuator.

***Motors (Right joystick):*** Moving joystick up/down moves robot forword/backwords, moving left/right turns robot left/right.

## Secondary controls

***Cameras (Start, Right joystick button):*** Clicking <u>start</u> switches camera views, holding the <u>right joystick button</u> toggles camera view reverting afterwords.

***Automations (Back+Trigger):*** Clicking <u>back+(X,A,B,Y,D-PAD)</u> will start any of the 8 saved automated functions. Note since this uses the motors and actuators the arm button must be held while running. 

***Robot direction (Back+Right joystick button):*** Clicking <u>back+right joystick button</u> will cause the robot to flip the motor direction to allow it to drive backwords.

***Servo (Back+Right joystick):*** Pressing <u>back+right joystick</u> will move the servo motor back and forth.

## Extra controls

***Fault reset (Left joystick button+Right joystick button):*** Clicking <u>left joystick button+right joystick button</u> will send a command to the robot reseting the softwere e-stop. 

***Component swap (Back+Start+Trigger):*** Clicking the<u>back+start+(X,A,B,Y)</u> will make the robot flip the direction of a single component flipping the arm actuators, bucket actuator, motor forword direction, or motor turning direction allowing the directions of major system components to be flipped.
